# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences, roughly equal weight:

- **Thai parents of EP/International students (ป.5–ม.3).** Their child learns science in English; the parent wants to help review at home but is not confident in English. They land on the home page, browse sheets, and get in touch to request one.
- **The tutor and their students in class.** The tutor teaches from lesson slides and pre-class basic checks on an iPad in landscape with an Apple Pencil, and reviews results on a phone or computer. Students take the basic checks before a lesson.

## Product Purpose
scitem.net is a tutor's bilingual (Thai–English) science site. It serves two jobs at once:

1. A teaching toolkit the tutor uses day to day: interactive lesson slides, basic checks and an answer vault.
2. A way to reach parents: showcase the bilingual sheets and get parents to ask for a sample or a class.

Success means parents understand what the sheets offer and get in touch, and in class the slides and checks work without friction on the iPad.

## Positioning
Sheets and lessons are built around the actual EP/Inter curriculum, in English with Thai translations, plus a parent's corner telling parents what to ask their child. Exercises use a "Code Breaker" mechanic: finishing all the exercises yields a code that unlocks the answers.

## Operating Context
- Home page (`src/pages/index.astro`): sheet list (`sheets`) and class links (`classLinks`) to slides and checks.
- Lesson slides (`public/demo/`, `public/light-p5/`): full-screen, with pen/eraser/palm rejection, ◀ ▶ paging and swipe, and tap-to-animate figures. They are used live in tutoring sessions on an iPad in landscape with an Apple Pencil.
- Basic checks (`/check/*`): students answer before a lesson. Answer keys are encrypted.
- Answer Vault (`/vault`): answers unlocked by code.
- Printed or PDF sheets (`public/sheets/*.pdf`) are the core material; slides are adapted from the scripts that generate the sheets.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Astro plus static HTML in `public/`. Pushing to `main` deploys to GitHub Pages through GitHub Actions.
- No new libraries: CSS and vanilla JS only.
- The repo is public, so answers must never appear in plain text in committed files. Use the encrypted check-keys or vault mechanisms.
- `public/light-p5/index.html` is generated by `tools/build-light-p5.py`. Never edit it directly.
- Primary in-class device: an iPad in landscape with an Apple Pencil, so touch and pen input come first. Teacher review happens on a phone or desktop.
- Content must match the real EP/Inter curriculum (binding).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: **scitem** (scitem.net).
- Bilingual: English science terms with Thai explanations. Thai is the language of the site's own copy.
- The current look (teal `#0f766e`, IBM Plex Sans Thai, warm off-white background) is the incumbent style. The user has not made it binding.

- No testimonials, student results, pricing or press exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Curriculum truth first: content follows the real EP/Inter syllabus, and design never bends it.
2. A parent with weak English must still understand and be able to help.
3. In class, nothing gets in the way: big touch targets, pen-friendly, fast, and works on an iPad in landscape.
4. Answers stay locked: they are earned through Code Breaker or encrypted keys, never exposed in source.
5. Lightweight and maintainable by one tutor: static files, no extra libraries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Bilingual readability: Thai and English mixed on one page must both render well (Thai font fallback).
- Students aged about 10–15; touch and pen input are primary in class.

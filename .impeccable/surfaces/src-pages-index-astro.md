## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is a periodic table of the tutor's material. Every lesson, check, sheet and tool is one element tile. It refuses the template of a hero, three icon cards and a contact band.
OWN-WORLD: A cool classroom-poster ground. Ink-ruled tiles: number at top-left, grade tag at top-right, a big two-letter symbol, the English name, then the Thai name and a meta "mass" line. Four family fills: parents coral, students yellow, lessons blue, teacher violet. Type is Anuphan, self-hosted.
STORY: A visitor sees one table, reads the key and knows which colour is theirs. They tap a family to get a page of only their tiles.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The table fills the width. The top-middle gap, where real posters put the key, holds the scitem title, a one-line pitch, an enlarged sample tile explaining its anatomy, and four family keys that are the navigation. The LINE action sits in the gap.
